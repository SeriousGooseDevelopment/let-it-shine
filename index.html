Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Not sure what your vehicle needs? Call John at (641) 780-4285 for a straightforward recommendation.

Do you coat more than cars? +

Yes. Ceramic coating is available for classic cars, motorcycles, farm equipment, aircraft, boats, RVs, and more. If it rolls, floats, or flies, we can talk protection.

What does a full detail include? +

A full detail includes deep interior cleaning of seats, carpets, dashboard, doors, and trunk, plus exterior wash, wax, polish, windows, wheels, and tires. Engine bay detailing is available on request.

How much does ceramic coating cost? +

Pricing depends on vehicle size, condition, paint correction needed, and coating package. Contact us for a customized quote.

Can you handle boats, RVs, or planes? +

Yes. Mobile detailing is available for large vehicles including planes, RVs, and boats. We deliver professional care on-site.

Do you offer window tinting and gift certificates? +

Yes. Window tinting helps with privacy, heat, UV protection, and appearance. Gift certificates are also available for birthdays, holidays, anniversaries, graduations, or any special occasion.
